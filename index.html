How to play
Left thumb slides sideways to steer ↺ ↻ – let go and the drill goes straight. Right thumb sets the speed from 0 to 100 % of the drill's normal top speed – it speeds up or brakes to hold it. Speed you build with perfect dives and combos goes on top at 100 %. The slider stays where you leave it.
Prefer the old way? Settings → Controls → Tap sides (hold a side to turn, both to brake).
The drill only bites when there's earth ahead – in caverns it falls like a rock. Soft ground caves in behind you.
In the air, keep holding to flip. Buy wings in the workshop to steer your flight. Land nose-first along the slope for a perfect dive; land sideways and you lie there until you rotate the nose down.
The earth eats your speed – pop out and fly to get it back. Old bombs blow you sky-high, a hot drill ignites gas, water cools you.
Spend gems in the Workshop. The Daily run is the same ground for everyone today – race your own ghost.

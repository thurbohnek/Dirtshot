How to play
Left thumb is a joystick: point it where you want the drill to go – let go and it keeps its heading. Right thumb sets the speed from 0 to 100 % of the drill's normal top speed. Speed you build with perfect dives and combos goes on top at 100 %.
Pinch with two fingers (or use + / −) to zoom; AUTO gives the camera back.
The drill only bites when there's earth ahead – in caverns it falls like a rock. Soft ground caves in behind you.
Land nose-first along the slope for a perfect dive. Buy wings to steer your flight.
Everything you find only counts once you bring it up to the surface – things from moons and comets must come home to the planet.
The earth eats your speed – pop out and fly to get it back. Old bombs blow you sky-high, a hot drill ignites gas, water cools you.
Spend gems in the Workshop, build the Rocket to reach new worlds. The Daily run is the same ground for everyone today.

# Dirtshot

An endless drill game by **Studio.grane**. A heavy metal drill falls from the sky, dives into the ground and drills tunnels through soil, clay, gravel, rock and magma.

## Play
Open `index.html` in a browser (mobile or desktop). Single file, no build step.

## Controls
- Hold the **right** side of the screen: rotate clockwise ↻
- Hold the **left** side: rotate counter-clockwise ↺
- Let go: the drill goes straight
- Keyboard: ←/→ (or A/D), P/Esc to pause

## Features
- Fixed-step physics (120 Hz), heavy drill with fins that pop out in the air
- Tiled destructible terrain with real chunk cave-ins, boulders, old bombs, gas, coal, water and lava
- Biomes: meadow → desert → tundra → volcano
- Old mine shafts, groundwater flow, fossils and treasures (collection)
- Workshop upgrades, missions, depth and distance records
- Daily run with ghost of your best run
- Day/night cycle, darkness and headlight underground
